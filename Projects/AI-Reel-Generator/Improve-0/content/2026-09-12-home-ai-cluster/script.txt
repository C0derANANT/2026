Your next AI server might already be at home.

NVIDIA just announced PAIR: a Personal AI Router that sends local AI requests across compatible computers on your network.

Think of an agent splitting research, coding and testing into separate jobs. Instead of every job waiting on one GPU, PAIR can route it to a PC with capacity.

That is a computer-engineering shift. VRAM, local networks, scheduling and privacy are AI skills now.

The future is not just bigger models. It is smaller AI systems working together.
