# Your Home PCs Can Now Share AI Work

One GPU is a bottleneck. A local network of machines can be an AI system.

NVIDIA PAIR is an open-source tool designed to route independent local-inference jobs across compatible PCs. For computer engineers, the interesting part is the stack underneath: VRAM, routing, latency, scheduling and privacy.

The future of AI may be less about a single massive machine—and more about small systems that cooperate.
